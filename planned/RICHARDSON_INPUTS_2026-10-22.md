## Implied concentration (our arithmetic)
At ~140 deliveries/day over 13 h, 10 h holds ~110 deliveries, i.e. ~220 passes (out and back). 122 passes is then **about half of all hub traffic over one home**. The uniform straight-line model gives about 5–10 % within 150 m of a home 0.5–1 km away.
