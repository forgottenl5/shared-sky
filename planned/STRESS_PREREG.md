# Stress events: a pop-up flight restriction and a rush-hour surge (study, written 2026-10-22 before any code or run)
Status: behaviour model of filed operations (planned tracks only), on PROC's plan check (first filed goes first, 30 m / ±5 s / 10 m) with VALLOC A2 heights and filed profiles. Not a forecast or safety assessment.

## Question
How do a temporary flight restriction (TFR) over a busy area and a short order surge change delays under a shared plan check, and how fast does the system recover?

## Setup
- d = 4 and 3 km; NEW seeds 16–18. Warm-up 1 h, measurement 2 h (as all PROC-family runs).
- **Stress window:** t = 5,400 to 7,200 s (the middle 30 min of the measurement).
- **After window:** t = 7,200 to 9,000 s (30 min).
- Flights are generated by proc.flights (same draws as PROC P2 / FAIR F0 for the base traffic).

## Arms
- **S0 — No stress.**
- **TFR — Pop-up restriction.** A cylinder of radius 1,000 m at the zone centre (0, 0), all heights, active during the window.
  - Flights launching in the window whose cruise legs would cross it are rerouted around it (shortest tangent–arc–tangent path, as nfz.js, on a circle 5 m outside).
  - Flights launching in the window whose pad or customer lies inside it are held until the window ends (start moved to 7,200 s).
  - Flights launched before the window are unchanged (limit: no in-flight rerouting).
- **SURGE — 3× orders.** Extra flights at twice each site's rate, with start times inside the window, are added to the base traffic (separate random stream: seed + 50,000). Total demand in the window is 3×.
- **BOTH — TFR + SURGE.**

## Measures (zone-touching flights, by start-time interval)
- Delayed share, mean delay of delayed flights, total delay (h) and cancellations, for flights starting in the window and in the after window.
- TFR: flights rerouted, held, and extra cruise distance. Delayed share of rerouted vs other flights in the window.

## Predictions (pooled over seeds 16–18)
- **R1:** SURGE: the delayed share of flights starting in the window is >= 2× S0's for the same interval, at d = 4 and at d = 3. PREDICTION: holds.
- **R2:** SURGE: the delayed share of flights starting in the after window is <= 1.5× S0's for the same interval (no lasting backlog), at d = 4 and at d = 3. PREDICTION: holds.
- **R3:** TFR: in the window, rerouted flights' delayed share is >= 2× that of the other flights starting in the window (detours bunch traffic at the cylinder edge), at d = 3. PREDICTION: holds.
- **R4 (reported only):** BOTH vs SURGE; held flights; extra distance; cancellations; mean delays.

No criterion or parameter changes after the data is in; at most two fix attempts on a failing feature check; deviations logged below. The analysis script is committed before any study run.

## Feature checks (before the study)
- **K1:** S0 reproduces FAIR F0 / PROC P2 delays exactly (d = 4, seeds 4–6).
- **K2:** In TFR, no cruise sample of a flight starting in the window lies inside the cylinder (tolerance 1 m) while it is active, except for held flights after their new start; held flights start at 7,200 s.
- **K3:** SURGE adds flights only with start times in the window, at about twice the base count of that window (ratio 1.7–2.3 at d = 3, seed 16).
- **K4:** Brute-force recheck of BOTH at d = 4, seed 16: 0 cross-operator conflicts.
