# ACCESS1b addendum (27 Oct 2026): all twelve Archer sites (descriptive, no predictions)
Same model and data as ACCESS1 (planned/ACCESS1_RESULTS_2026-10-27.md), with Frisco Station (DFW) and the Thompson Hotel helipad (Houston) added. Coordinates hand-read by the founder; Thompson matches OurAirports heliport 17TT within about 30 m; the Frisco point is inside the development, the pad location is not public.

- **Frisco Station adds about a fifth to DFW** (16,500 more jobs), and more than doubles the off-peak share. Its pairs with Fort Worth Meacham and Arlington are now among the largest (Frisco-Meacham both ways 19,900). It also makes the registered P1 range (at most 3 %) fail for DFW with twelve sites; P1 was registered on ten sites, so the registered verdict stands and this is reported here.
- **The downtown Houston helipad adds many people nearby but almost no time-savers** (+900 jobs). About 900,000 residents live within 15 minutes of it, about 730,000 of them not already within 15 minutes of another site, but downtown is about 12 km from Hobby, so most trips through it are short enough to drive. Its only sizeable pair is Galveston to downtown (3,100 jobs).
- **Fragility is unchanged in kind** (access legs slower than the corridor speed): DFW 3.05 % at the registered speed, 1.59 % at 80 %, 0.45 % at 60 %; Houston 1.28 %, 0.56 %, 0.35 %. Fair range with twelve sites: about 0.5-3 % (DFW), 0.3-1.3 % (Houston).
- No income skew still: share of jobs over $3,333 a month among beneficiaries 58.5 % (DFW, all jobs 59.9 %), 52.3 % (Houston, 57.2 %).
