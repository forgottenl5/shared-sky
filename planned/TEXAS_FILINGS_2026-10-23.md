# Texas FAA filings, read directly (2026-10-23)
Leads came from a Gemini report (Texas_Drone_Delivery_Operations_Analysis.pdf, founder-supplied); every figure below was read in the FAA document itself. Gemini's other claims (incidents, lawsuits, Part 108 status) are not used.

## Amazon Prime Air — Final Texas EA (multi-metro), January 2026
Source: [FINAL Texas EA Main Report](https://www.faa.gov/uas/advanced_operations/nepa_and_drones/FINAL_Texas_EA_Main_Report.pdf); FONSI/ROD: [FONSI ROD Amazon Multi Metro TX](https://www.faa.gov/uas/advanced_operations/nepa_and_drones/FONSI_ROD_Amazon_Multi_Metro_TX_Signed.pdf). Comment period 15 Aug – 1 Oct 2025; the copy read has blank signature lines (FONSI/ROD exists separately).
- En route height: "Aircraft would typically fly en route at between approximately 180 and 377 ft AGL" (Sec. 2.2.3, p. 2-2; same in Sec. 3.3.3, p. 3-12). **55–115 m, one band for both legs.** Sec. 3.3.3.2 (p. 3-14) contradicts this with "(150 to 300 feet AGL)".
- Maximum: "can fly up to 400 ft above ground level" (Sec. 2.2.2). Return: "climbs vertically and follows its predefined route back to the PADDC at its assigned altitude" (Sec. 2.2.3).
- Delivery: lowers to about 13 ft AGL, hovers 2 s (Sec. 3.3.3.2).
- Volume and range: up to 1,000 flights per site per operating day, 7 am–10 pm; 7.5 mi range, 174 sq mi (Sec. 1.2).
- 22 sites. **Houston (6):** SAH1 1202 E FM 1960 Rd, Houston 77073; SAHx NW corner Beltway 8 / Hwy 90, Missouri City 77489; STX5 5215 Campbell Rd, Houston 77041; HOU2 10550 Ella Blvd, Houston 77038; HOU6 10507 Harlem Rd, Richmond 77407; STX4 10611 Red Bluff Rd, Pasadena 77507. **DFW (7):** STX2 Carrollton; STX3 Dallas (Rock Quarry Rd); STX8 3051 Research Dr, Richardson; DAL3 Dallas (Chalk Hill Rd); IAH1 Dallas (Southlink Dr); DFW7 Fort Worth/Haslet; FTW4 Fort Worth (Heritage Pkwy). Also San Antonio 3, Austin 3, El Paso 1, Waco 1, San Marcos 1.

**What this changes:** our "filed" Amazon heights (61 m out, 105 m back) came from the Omaha EA's representative noise profile. The Texas filing that actually covers Houston gives a 55–115 m band for both legs, which contains Zipline's 101 m. The 4 m gap is one possible choice inside Amazon's Texas band, not the filed Texas plan. Where Amazon flies inside the band is not stated; local Richardson reports suggest about 69 m out and near 122 m back (122 m is above the 115 m band top).

## Wing — Houston EA, 2026
Draft read: [Draft EA, May 2026](https://www.faa.gov/uas/advanced_operations/nepa_and_drones/20260504_Full_Draft_EA_Wing_Houston_for_Publication_Compiled.pdf). Final: [Final EA, 13 Aug 2026](https://www.faa.gov/uas/advanced_operations/nepa_and_drones/20260813_Final_EA_Wing_Houston_ADA_Signed.pdf) (not machine-readable to us) and [FONSI/ROD](https://www.faa.gov/uas/advanced_operations/nepa_and_drones/20260813_Wing_Houston_FONSI-ROD_Signed.pdf).
- Cruise: "generally be operated at an altitude of 150–300 feet above ground level" (Sec. 2.2.2, p. 2-8); Sec. 3.4.3.2 says 150–250 ft. Always below 400 ft.
- Delivery: descends to a stationary hover at 23 ft AGL (Sec. 2.2.2).
- Up to 75 nests in Houston; up to 400 flights per nest per day; about 30,000 per day metro-wide at maximum; 6 mi radius (Sec. 1.1, 2.2, 3.4.3). Hummingbird 7000W-B and 8000-A.
- Matches the model (46–91 m cruise, 7 m hover, 400/day per nest).

## Zipline — Texas Metros Draft EA, 11 Sep 2026
Source: [Draft EA](https://www.faa.gov/uas/advanced_operations/nepa_and_drones/20260911_Zipline_Texas_Metros_Draft_EA_CLEAN_ADA_Signed.pdf). Draft; 30-day comment period from 11 Sep 2026. Covers Houston, San Antonio, Austin, Amarillo, El Paso (not DFW).
- Houston: 65 Chargers, 1,300 Dropboxes, up to 65,000 deliveries per day (Table 2.2-1); up to 1,000 per Charger per day (p. 2-2). **20 Dropboxes per Charger**, as the model assumed.
- Cruise and hover at about 330 ft AGL (101 m) (p. 2-8); 70 mph; pick-up hover 120 s, delivery hover 75 s (pp. 2-9, 2-10). Round trip at most 20 miles (p. 2-3). 24/7, about 95 % between 7 am and 10 pm.
- Matches the model. Dropbox distance from Charger not stated (model: within 5 km).
