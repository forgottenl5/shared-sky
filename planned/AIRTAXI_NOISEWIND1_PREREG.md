# AIRTAXI_NOISEWIND1: does departing and landing into the real wind change who hears the air taxis? (registration, 29 Oct 2026)

Status: scenario model of planned operations. Registered before the runner, analysis or any run. Closes the second half of GAP_PLAN A7 (wind-aligned departure paths), which AIRTAXI_NOISE2 left out.

## Question
AIRTAXI_NOISE1 and NOISE2 assume every climb-out and final approach points along the straight line to or from the other site, because real departure paths are not public. Real aircraft take off and land into the wind. Does using the real wind change the noise footprint or the number of people under it?

## Method
- Flights, noise model, grid, windows, census population, off-site rule: exactly as AIRTAXI_NOISE2 (taxi/net5.js with the 3D-page settings, taxi/noise.js, 200 m grid, Census 2020 block population, cells more than 300 m from every site), window **06:00-18:00 only**, seeds 1-5, the six metro-levels (DFW and Houston, 1.5 / 3 / 6 requests an hour per route direction).
- Two modes per run, same flights: **line** (as NOISE2: ground track along the line to the other site) and **wind** (new). taxi/noise.js gets one optional hook (`Grid.posFn`, default unchanged) so the runner can supply the wind position function; the default path is byte-for-byte the NOISE2 behaviour (check K1).
- Wind mode: the take-off climb-out (the first `dc` metres of ground track, `dc` as in noise.js `prof`) flies on the heading **into the wind**, the unit vector toward the bearing the wind comes from; the final approach flies the same heading, so the aircraft arrives from the downwind side of the site and the descent ground track ends at the site. The cruise leg stays on the line between the sites, and the connection between the cruise leg and the climb-out or approach legs is not modelled (the noise sources simply switch position). Flight times, energy and every other number are unchanged. Hover over the site and holding are unchanged.
- Wind: real hourly 80 m wind direction for 2025 from the project's own Open-Meteo files (data/openmeteo_dfw_2025.json, data/openmeteo_houston_2025.json; times are UTC, converted to America/Chicago local time). For each seed, one real day is drawn at random from the weekdays of 2025 with complete 06:00-18:00 data (mulberry32 seeded with seed x 7919 plus 17 for DFW and plus 31 for Houston); the flight uses the direction of the local hour in which the take-off (climb-out) or landing (approach) happens. No wind-speed cut-off: calm hours use the recorded direction.
- Measures per run: peak off-site DNL; people in cells with N55 >= 10 and N55 >= 50 flights over the window; people at DNL >= 55 and >= 65; **off-site cells with N55 >= 1** (footprint area, count of 200 m cells).

## Predictions (written before any run)
- **P1:** no off-site cell reaches DNL 65 in any run, either mode.
- **P2:** the median peak off-site DNL differs between wind and line by less than 1.5 dB in all six metro-levels.
- **P3:** the median people with at least 10 loud flights differ by more than 20% (either direction) between wind and line in at least 3 of the six metro-levels.
- **P4:** nobody lives at DNL >= 55 in any metro-level in wind mode (median people at DNL >= 55 is 0 everywhere).
- **P5:** the median number of off-site cells with N55 >= 1 is larger in wind mode than in line mode in at least 5 of the six metro-levels (flights spread over more directions).

## Checks before the study runs (K)
- K1: line mode seed 1 reproduces the 3D page's noise layer exactly (the same cells and counts as taxi/airtaxi_data.json) in all six metro-levels, so the hook changes nothing by default.
- K2: with a fixed wind from bearing 90 (east), the wind-mode climb-out samples move east of the site and the approach samples sit west of the site at 3 km before touchdown; with wind from 0 (north) the climb-out moves north.
- K3: both wind files have 8,760 hourly rows with no missing direction; at least 200 weekdays have all twelve local hours 06:00-17:59 present; the local-hour conversion gives 06:00 local = 11:00 UTC on 15 July and 12:00 UTC on 15 January.
