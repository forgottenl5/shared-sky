# REALCOORD results (23 Nov 2026): the plan-sharing cost on real Walmart site layouts
Registration planned/REALCOORD_PREREG.md (fingerprint in planned/REALCOORD_PREREG.md.sha256; runner and analysis committed before any run). K1-K3 passed. 80 runs, all completed. Table: planned/realcoord_results_table.md (generated by planned/realcoord_analyze.py). No deviations.

## Result in one line
**On real Walmart site layouts the new headline holds: giving each company its own height roughly halves how many flights the pre-flight check holds (Houston 34.1 % -> 15.8 %, DFW 27.6 % -> 14.9 %; ratio 2.16 and 1.85), and the check removes 98.6-100 % of flown planned conflicts.** 4 of 4 predictions passed.

## Predictions
- **W1 PASS** A2 + V2 delayed share: Houston 15.8 % (95 % 14.6-16.9), DFW 14.9 % (14.3-15.5); registered 12-26 % / 11-24 %.
- **W2 PASS** A0 / A2 delayed-share ratio: Houston 2.16 (2.05-2.29), DFW 1.85 (1.80-1.92); registered 1.5-2.6.
- **W3 PASS** V2 cuts flown planned conflicts against no plan sharing by 99.9 / 100.0 % (Houston A0 / A2) and 99.3 / 98.6 % (DFW).
- **W4 PASS** without plan sharing, flown in wind, own heights cut planned conflicts by 77.5 % (Houston, 746 -> 168) and 83.5 % (DFW, 430 -> 71) against the Omaha profile.

## Reading
- Compared with the random-site layouts (SEEDS2: 18.9 % vs 38.8 % Houston, 17.5 % vs 36.5 % DFW), real Walmart layouts hold fewer flights in both height plans, and the halving holds (DFW slightly less than half: 1.85x). The random-site figures used on the public pages are therefore, if anything, the higher estimate of the cost.
- **Amazon bears most of the waiting, with either height plan.** About 71-84 % of Amazon's flights are held (Houston A0 81.5 %, A2 84.1 %; DFW 74.7 % / 71.1 %), against 5-23 % for Wing and 15-36 % for Zipline. The same pattern was in COORD1 / SEEDS2 (Amazon 81-84 %) and is explained in the COORD1 diagnosis: Amazon's long climbs and descents at its depots cross every other company's reserved layer, and the shared-out gaps (10-14 m) are smaller than the check's reservation (+-10 m). Height sharing halves the total because it cuts Wing's and Zipline's waits by half to three quarters; it does not help Amazon. COORD2 showed that sizing the gaps to the reservation brings Amazon to 36-64 %.
- Mean delay of held flights 5.8-6.6 minutes, as in SEEDS2.

## Limits
- Same check model as COORD1 / SEEDS2 (V2 volumes, plans on the previous hour's wind, windy hours at the 85th-95th percentile); not any operator's real system.
- REALSITES layouts: Wing grown onto the nearest Walmarts from its real stores (DFW Zipline too); Houston Zipline is still random around its Cypress area; at full build-out 16-34 sites per metro are random because stores run out. REALSITES' Houston zone (84 x 84 km) is larger than the headline layout's (68 x 68 km), so Houston absolute counts are not comparable with SEEDS2 (DFW uses the same 80 x 80 km zone); shares and ratios are.
