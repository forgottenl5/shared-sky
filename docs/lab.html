Findings and methods: what the simulation found (the full study record of registrations, code and results is in the public repository) · Houston simulation · Dallas–Fort Worth simulation. The lab engine is the research engine in one file; it reproduces the published counts exactly at the research settings.

A "planned conflict" is two planned flights of different companies within 30 m horizontally and 10 m vertically at the same moment, checked four times a second. It measures how plans interact with no coordination between companies, not collision risk. Pass counts include some predictions that were easy to meet; the failures are the informative part. Planned tracks only: no wind, no in-flight avoidance.
